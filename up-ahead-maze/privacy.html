Up Ahead Maze.

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Overview

Up Ahead Maze (the "App") is an offline, single-player game for young children. This policy explains how the App handles information. In short: the App does not collect any personal information.

Information we collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers. It does not require an account, registration, or an internet connection.

Children's privacy

The App is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users.

Advertising and analytics

The App does not include third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics services. There are no in-app purchases.

Game data

Game progress and drawings may be stored locally on your device. This data stays on the device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the App removes this data.

Third-party services

The App does not use third-party services that collect user information.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above.
